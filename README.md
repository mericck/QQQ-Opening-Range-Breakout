# QQQ Opening Range Breakout Research

A quantitative research project investigating the performance of a
15-minute Opening Range Breakout strategy on QQQ.

The project first evaluates whether the strategy exhibits a persistent
trading edge and subsequently investigates its suitability for
proprietary trading firm challenge environments.

## Baseline Strategy

- Instrument: QQQ
- Opening Range: 09:30–09:45 ET
- Breakout Confirmation: 5-minute candle close
- Entry: Next 5-minute candle open
- Stop Loss: Opposite side of the opening range
- Profit Target: 2R
- Entry Cutoff: 12:00 ET
- Forced Exit: 15:00 ET
- Maximum Trades: 1 per day

## Research Design

**Development / In-Sample**

2017–2022

**Out-of-Sample**

2023–2026

The out-of-sample period is reserved as a holdout dataset and will not
be used to modify the baseline strategy parameters.
